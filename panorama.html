  1. 1

    100%-Fill Ad Waterfall & Frequency Capping (services/panorama-edge)

    Every preroll, midroll, or postroll slot runs through a deterministic waterfall: Creator Manifest → Moderated UGC Ads → Panorama Auction → VAST/VMAP Fallback → Streamy House Promo. Per-viewer hourly frequency caps prevent ad fatigue, and every decision returns a signed decisionToken, Ad Pod countdown metadata, and a Second-Screen QR URL.

  2. 2

    Signed Playback Telemetry & Proof-of-Impression (panorama-proof-worker)

    PanoramaPlaybackOrchestrator on Android TV tracks start, quartile, and complete milestones with durable local queue persistence for spotty Wi-Fi. Events are HMAC-signed at the edge and verified by Panorama's prover/verifier lane before any payout intent becomes settleable.

  3. 3

    Trust & Anti-Fraud Gate (services/settlement-bridge + services/trust-api)

    Client TV devices can never spoof a payout destination: settlement-bridge resolves the authoritative channelId server-side and checks trust-api for active fraudHold flags before recording pending micro-USDC accruals.

  4. 4

    Native Solana & EVM USDC MultiSplitter Settlement (services/payouts)

    Because Panorama Market runs natively on Solana (SVM), Streamy settles directly in Solana SPL USDC with zero cross-chain bridge—or routes to Ethereum / Base ERC20 USDC for EVM creators. Co-creators and channel teams claim their exact proportional share in O(1) constant time.
