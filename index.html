How Streamy Works:

  2. 2

    Democratized Ads via Panorama Protocol

    Streamy pairs directly with Panorama's Proof-of-Impression pipeline, frequency-capped waterfall (creator manifests, UGC ads, auction, VAST fallback), and Second-Screen QR offers—no creepy cross-device tracking or opaque middlemen.

    Verified ad revenue settles in USDC straight into solo creator wallets or constant-time O(1) collaborator splitters on Solana or Ethereum / Base—while the TV stick stays 100% read-only with zero keys on hardware.
